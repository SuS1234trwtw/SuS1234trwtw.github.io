Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains what the Focus iPhone app ("Focus", "we") stores and why. Focus has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data.

Tasks and focus sessions

Your tasks and finished focus/break sessions are stored on your phone. They are also synced to our database (hosted by Supabase) under an anonymous account the app creates automatically, so they can be restored. We don't ask for your name or email for this.

Spotify (optional)

If you connect Spotify, Focus reads the song currently playing and can play, pause and skip on your request. Your Spotify sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's Keychain and are only sent to Spotify. We don't store your Spotify data on our servers.

Google Calendar (optional)

If you connect Google Calendar, Focus asks for the calendar.app.created permission. With it, Focus:

  • creates one secondary calendar named "Focus" in your Google account, and
  • adds an event to that calendar for each finished focus block (and break, if you enable it), containing the start time, end time and the name of the task you were working on.

Focus cannot read, change or delete any of your other calendars or events. Your Google sign-in tokens are kept in your phone's Keychain and are only sent to Google. Event data goes directly from your phone to Google; we don't copy it to our servers, share it with anyone, or use it for anything other than creating those events.

Focus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • Unlink Spotify or Google Calendar in the app's Settings; this deletes the tokens from your phone.
  • You can revoke Focus's access to Google anytime at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the "Focus" calendar in Google Calendar.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on your phone. To delete synced tasks from our database, open an issue at the support link below.

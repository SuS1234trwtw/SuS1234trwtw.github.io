Focus

A Pomodoro timer and task list for iPhone, styled like a terminal.

What it does

  • Focus and break timer with custom lengths, sounds and haptics
  • A simple task list, synced across your devices
  • Live countdown in the Dynamic Island and on the lock screen
  • Optional: see and control what's playing on Spotify
  • Optional: log each finished focus block to a "Focus" calendar in your Google Calendar

Google Calendar

When you connect Google Calendar, Focus creates one calendar named "Focus" and adds an event to it each time you finish a focus block (and breaks, if you turn that on). Focus can only see and change calendars it created itself — it can't read your other calendars or events. You can unlink at any time in Settings.

Focus

A minimal Pomodoro timer and task list for iPhone, with a terminal-inspired look.

  • Focus & break timerCustom lengths, sounds and haptics.
  • Task listSimple tasks, synced across your devices.
  • Dynamic IslandLive countdown on the island and lock screen.
  • SpotifyOptional: see and control what’s playing.
  • Google CalendarOptional: log finished focus blocks to a “Focus” calendar.
  • Open sourceCode available under the MIT License.

How Focus uses Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. When you connect, Focus creates one secondary calendar named “Focus” in your Google account and adds an event to it each time you finish a focus block (and each break, if you turn that on). Focus requests only the calendar.app.created permission, so it can access only the calendar it created — it cannot see, change or delete your other calendars or events. You can unlink at any time in the app’s Settings.

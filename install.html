Install Focus

Updated October 1, 2026 · iPhone with iOS 26 or later · free Apple Account

Before you start

  • Focus isn’t on the App Store. You install it yourself (“sideloading”) with a free Apple Account.
  • Free installs expire after 7 days. Re-install with iloader, or let SideStore refresh it for you.
  • Your iPhone needs a passcode, and you need a USB cable for the first setup.
  • Download the latest Focus IPA: GitHub Releases → latest (the file named FocusTimer-<version>-<build>.ipa).

1. Which method should I pick?

  • iloader only — simplest. Plug in your phone and install the IPA from your computer. Every 7 days (and for each update) you do it again.
  • SideStore — a bit more setup once, then Focus updates and refreshes right on your phone, no computer needed. You still use iloader once to install SideStore.

2. iloader on Windows

  1. Check your PC. iloader needs 64-bit Windows on an Intel or AMD processor (Windows on Arm isn’t supported).
  2. Install iTunes so Windows can talk to your iPhone: download it from apple.com (recommended) or the Microsoft Store. Open it once and check it sees your iPhone. If it doesn’t, uninstall iTunes and “Apple Mobile Device Support”, then install the Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store instead.
  3. Download iloader only from github.com/nab138/iloader/releases or iloader.app. Pick iloader-windows-x64.msi and run the installer.
  4. Plug in your iPhone and unlock it. Tap Trust on the “Trust This Computer?” pop-up and enter your passcode.
  5. Sign in: open iloader. In the Apple ID panel enter your Apple Account email and password and click Login. Type the 6-digit code from your other Apple device and click Submit. (A different Apple Account than the one on your phone is fine. Security-key sign-in isn’t supported.)
  6. Select your iPhone in the iDevice panel. Not there? Click Refresh Devices.
  7. Install Focus: under Installers click Import IPA and choose the Focus .ipa you downloaded. Wait for “Sign & Install App” to finish. If iloader says “Maximum certificates reached”, let it revoke the old one and click Continue.
  8. Continue with First launch on the iPhone.

3. iloader on Mac

  1. Nothing extra to install — macOS already includes what’s needed to talk to your iPhone.
  2. Download iloader from github.com/nab138/iloader/releases or iloader.app: pick iloader-darwin-universal.dmg (works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs). Open it and drag iloader to Applications.
  3. If macOS blocks it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  4. Plug in your iPhone, unlock it and tap Trust on the phone (and on the Mac if asked). No prompt? Open Finder, then unplug and re-plug the phone.
  5. Then follow steps 5–8 of the Windows guide — iloader works the same on Mac.

4. First launch on the iPhone

  1. Trust the developer: open Settings → General → VPN & Device Management, tap your Apple Account under “Developer App”, tap Trust (then Allow & Restart if asked) and enter your passcode.
  2. Turn on Developer Mode: open Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, turn on Developer Mode and tap Restart. After the restart, confirm with Turn On and enter your passcode.
  3. Open Focus. Start a focus block to see the timer in the Dynamic Island.

5. SideStore: one-tap updates on the phone

  1. Install LocalDevVPN from the App Store. Open it, tap connect and allow the VPN configuration. SideStore needs it (and Wi-Fi) switched on whenever it installs, updates or refreshes apps.
  2. Install SideStore with iloader: plug in your phone, sign in and select it in iloader (steps 1–6 of the Windows guide), then under Installers choose SideStore (Stable). iloader downloads SideStore, installs it and places the pairing file for you.
  3. On the iPhone, trust the developer and turn on Developer Mode (section 4), connect LocalDevVPN, open SideStore and sign in with the same Apple Account.
  4. In SideStore, go to My Apps and tap the 7 DAYS button next to SideStore to refresh it once (tap Yes or Refresh Now if asked). SideStore may jump to the Home Screen — that’s normal.
  5. Add the Focus source: open the Sources tab, tap +, paste this URL and tap Done:
    https://github.com/SuS1234trwtw/focus-timer/releases/latest/download/source.json
    Already have Focus installed? Open Focus → Settings → updates → add to sidestore instead.
  6. Open the Focus source and install it. When SideStore asks about App Extensions, choose Keep App Extensions (Register App ID for Each Extension) so the Dynamic Island and widgets keep working.
  7. SideStore now refreshes Focus in the background and shows an Update button when a new build is out.

6. Updating Focus

  • Focus checks for new builds by itself. When one is out you’ll see > update available at the top of the app and in Settings → updates.
  • iloader: download the new IPA from Releases on your computer, then use Import IPA again. Install over the old version (don’t delete it first) so your local settings stay; tasks are also backed up by sync.
  • SideStore: open My Apps and tap Update next to Focus.

7. Troubleshooting

  • iloader can’t find my iPhone — make sure iTunes (or Apple Devices) sees it, try another cable or USB port, unlock the phone and tap Trust.
  • “Untrusted Developer” when opening Focus — do step 1 of section 4.
  • “Unable to Verify App” — connect to the internet, then in VPN & Device Management tap your Apple Account and Verify App.
  • Focus stopped opening after a week — the free 7-day signature expired. Re-install with iloader, or refresh it in SideStore.
  • “Maximum number of apps” / not enough App IDs — a free account can have 3 sideloaded apps active at once (SideStore counts as one; expired apps still count) and register 10 App IDs per 7 days. Remove an app you don’t use, or wait for old App IDs to expire.
  • No timer in the Dynamic Island / no widget — the widget extension wasn’t signed. Re-install with iloader, or in SideStore pick “Register App ID for Each Extension”. Focus → Settings → island shows whether the extension is signed.
  • SideStore errors about the VPN, Wi-Fi or pairing file — turn on Wi-Fi and LocalDevVPN, turn off DNS or ad blockers, and restart SideStore. Still failing? In iloader click Delete Stored Pairing, re-select your phone and tap Trust, then Manage Pairing File → Place next to SideStore. See the SideStore error codes.
